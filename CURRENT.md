Three ideas: (1) **Routing is a state-decision, not navigation** — the whole topic falls out of choosing the URL as the source of truth for a slice of app state; the URL is *external, serializable, addressable* state the browser and user can act on, where a top-level `useState` "current page" is memory-only.
