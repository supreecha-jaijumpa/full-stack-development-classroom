> Goal: Ship a production-grade full-stack product, start to finish

| M18 | Capstone Deploy & Harden | 🔒 | — | — |
| M18 | Capstone Presentation & Review | 🔒 | — | — |

**Capstone (Final Milestone):** Design, build, test, deploy, and present a production-grade full-stack application of your own choosing. It must include: authenticated users, a real database, a tested frontend and backend, CI/CD, monitoring, and a written design doc. Present it as if defending it in a senior engineering interview — architecture, trade-offs, what you'd do differently at 100x scale.

> Pass this, and you can credibly call yourself a senior-capable full-stack engineer. The portfolio piece is the proof.
