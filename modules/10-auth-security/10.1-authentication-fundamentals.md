# 10.1 Authentication Fundamentals

> Module 10 — Authentication & Security · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** What it means to prove identity and how authentication differs from authorization.

- Authentication vs. authorization (who you are vs. what you may do)
- Credentials, factors, and multi-factor authentication
- The login lifecycle: identify, verify, establish state
- Where auth state lives: cookies, headers, and tokens
- Logout, expiry, and revoking access
- Common attack surfaces in login flows

**Hands-on:** Build registration and login endpoints that verify a credential and return a session or token.
**You can move on when:** You can clearly explain the difference between authentication and authorization with examples.
