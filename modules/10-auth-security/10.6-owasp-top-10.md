# 10.6 OWASP Top 10

> Module 10 — Authentication & Security · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** The most common web application security risks and how to defend against them.

- Injection (SQL and others) and parameterized queries
- Broken access control and authentication failures
- Cryptographic failures and sensitive data exposure
- Security misconfiguration and vulnerable dependencies
- Cross-site scripting (XSS) and cross-site request forgery (CSRF)
- Logging, monitoring, and server-side request forgery (SSRF)

**Hands-on:** Audit your API against the OWASP Top 10 and remediate at least two concrete findings.
**You can move on when:** You can name each Top 10 category and a mitigation for it.
