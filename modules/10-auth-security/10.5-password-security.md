# 10.5 Password Security

> Module 10 — Authentication & Security · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to store and handle passwords so a breach doesn't expose them.

- Why you never store plaintext or reversible passwords
- Hashing vs. encryption and why slow hashes matter
- Salts, pepper, and per-password uniqueness
- Modern algorithms: bcrypt, scrypt, argon2
- Password policies, breach lists, and rate limiting login attempts
- Secure password reset and email verification flows

**Hands-on:** Hash passwords on registration with bcrypt or argon2 and verify them on login.
**You can move on when:** Stored credentials are salted hashes and login verifies without ever comparing plaintext.
