# 14.7 Deploy a Full-Stack App (hands-on)

> Module 14 — DevOps & Deployment · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to bring containers, CI/CD, secrets, and monitoring together into one live deployment.

- Dockerizing the full stack with Compose for local parity
- Wiring a CI/CD pipeline that tests then deploys on push to main
- Deploying live with a managed database and proper secrets
- Running migrations safely as part of deploy
- Confirming logging/monitoring works against the live app

**Hands-on:** Deploy your full-stack app live via an automated pipeline with secrets and monitoring in place.
**You can move on when:** A push to main automatically tests and deploys your app to a working live URL.
