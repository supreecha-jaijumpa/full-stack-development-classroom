# 14.6 Logging & Monitoring

> Module 14 — DevOps & Deployment · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to see what your app is doing in production before users report problems.

- Structured logging with levels and request context
- Centralized logs and how to read them on your host
- Health check endpoints and uptime monitoring
- Error tracking (e.g. Sentry) and alerting basics
- Key metrics to watch: error rate, latency, and resource use

**Hands-on:** Add structured logging and a health check / basic uptime monitor.
**You can move on when:** You can observe requests and errors of your live app from logs and a health check.
