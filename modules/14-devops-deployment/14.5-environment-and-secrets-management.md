# 14.5 Environment & Secrets Management

> Module 14 — DevOps & Deployment · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to keep configuration and secrets out of your code and safe across environments.

- Separating config by environment (dev, staging, production)
- `.env` files, `.gitignore`, and never committing secrets
- Platform secret stores and CI/CD secret injection
- Rotating credentials and limiting secret scope
- Validating required env vars at startup so misconfig fails fast

**Hands-on:** Move all configuration to environment variables and store secrets in the platform's secret store.
**You can move on when:** No secret appears in your repo and the app reads all config from the environment.
