# 12.1 Backend Testing Strategy

> Module 12 — Backend Testing · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to decide what to test and at which level so effort goes where risk lives.

- The testing pyramid (and trophy) for backends
- Unit vs. integration vs. end-to-end, and the cost of each
- Identifying critical paths worth covering
- Why 100% coverage is a vanity metric
- What not to test (framework code, trivial getters)
- Arranging tests with the Arrange-Act-Assert pattern

**Hands-on:** Map your API's critical paths and assign each to a unit or integration test.
**You can move on when:** You can justify the test level for each part of your API in terms of risk.
