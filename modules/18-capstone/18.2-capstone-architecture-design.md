# 18.2 Capstone Architecture & Design Doc

> Module 18 — Capstone · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to turn your scoped capstone into a design doc that justifies every major technical decision.

- Drawing the architecture: clients, services, database, external integrations
- Designing the data model and Prisma schema for your domain
- Justifying the stack (React+TS, Node/Express or Next.js, PostgreSQL)
- Auth strategy and the security boundaries of the system
- Stating trade-offs and alternatives you rejected
- Defining what "done" and "production-ready" mean for this project

**Hands-on:** Write the capstone design doc: architecture diagram, data model, tech-stack justification, and key trade-offs.
**You can move on when:** Your design doc lets a reviewer understand and challenge your architecture before you write code.
