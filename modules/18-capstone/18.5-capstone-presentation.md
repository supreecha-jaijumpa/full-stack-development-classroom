# 18.5 Capstone Presentation & Review

> Module 18 — Capstone · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to present and defend your capstone the way you would in a senior engineering interview.

- Telling the project story: problem, users, and what you built
- Walking through the architecture and the decisions behind it
- Owning trade-offs and known limitations honestly
- Answering "what would you do differently at 100x scale?"
- Demoing the live app smoothly without surprises
- Taking stress-test questions and feedback gracefully

**Hands-on:** Present the project as a senior interview defense, covering architecture, trade-offs, and what you'd do at 100x scale.
**You can move on when:** You can present the system, justify your choices, and field hard questions about scale and failure without losing the thread.
