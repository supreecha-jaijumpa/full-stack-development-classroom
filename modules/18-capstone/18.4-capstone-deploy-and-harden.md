# 18.4 Capstone Deploy & Harden

> Module 18 — Capstone · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to get your capstone running in production and toughened against real-world failure and abuse.

- Containerizing with Docker and configuring environments
- Setting up a CI/CD pipeline that tests and deploys on merge
- Managing secrets and environment configuration safely
- Adding monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Security hardening: input validation, authz checks, rate limiting, headers
- Database migrations and backups in a deployed environment

**Hands-on:** Deploy via CI/CD with monitoring and logging in place, and complete a security/hardening pass.
**You can move on when:** Your app is live, deploys automatically on merge, and you can show monitoring plus a completed hardening checklist.
