# 15.5 Load Testing

> Module 15 — Performance & Scalability · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to find out what your app does under heavy, concurrent traffic.

- Load vs. stress vs. soak testing
- Writing scenarios with a tool like k6, Artillery, or Locust
- Key metrics: throughput, latency percentiles (p95/p99), and error rate
- Finding the breaking point and where it fails
- Comparing results before and after optimizations

**Hands-on:** Run a load test against your app and capture throughput and latency under load.
**You can move on when:** You can report your app's p95 latency and error rate at a target request rate.
