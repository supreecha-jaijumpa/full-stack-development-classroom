# 15.6 Profiling & Finding Bottlenecks

> Module 15 — Performance & Scalability · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to locate the real bottleneck with data instead of guessing.

- Profiling the backend (CPU, memory, flame graphs) and the frontend (React Profiler, DevTools)
- Distinguishing CPU-bound, I/O-bound, and network-bound problems
- Measuring before changing and isolating one variable at a time
- Reading traces and timelines to find the true hot path
- Documenting before/after numbers to prove the fix

**Hands-on:** Profile the app, identify the top bottleneck, fix it, and record before/after numbers.
**You can move on when:** You can point to profiling data that identifies and confirms a fixed bottleneck.
