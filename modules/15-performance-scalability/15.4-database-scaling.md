# 15.4 Database Scaling

> Module 15 — Performance & Scalability · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to keep PostgreSQL fast as data and traffic grow.

- Reading query plans with `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`
- Designing and adding indexes that actually help
- Rewriting slow queries and fixing N+1 patterns in Prisma
- Connection pooling and read replicas
- When to consider partitioning, sharding, or denormalization

**Hands-on:** Add indexes and rewrite the slowest query, measuring the change with `EXPLAIN`.
**You can move on when:** Your slowest query runs measurably faster with a justified index or rewrite.
