# 15.2 Backend Performance

> Module 15 — Performance & Scalability · Phase 4

**What you'll learn:** How to make your Go/Gin backend handle requests faster and more efficiently.

- Finding slow endpoints with timing and request metrics
- Goroutine leaks, lock contention, and limiting concurrency
- Reducing allocations and garbage collection pressure
- Reducing redundant and N+1 GORM queries
- Tuning the `database/sql` connection pool behind GORM
- Pagination, batching, and trimming response payloads

**Hands-on:** Find and fix a slow backend endpoint by reducing unnecessary queries, allocations, or contention.
**You can move on when:** A targeted endpoint's response time drops measurably after your change.
