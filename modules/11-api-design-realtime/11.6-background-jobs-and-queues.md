# 11.6 Background Jobs & Queues

> Module 11 — API Design & Real-Time · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to move slow or unreliable work out of the request/response cycle.

- Why offload work: latency, reliability, and spikes
- Job queues and workers (e.g. BullMQ on Redis)
- Producing jobs and processing them asynchronously
- Retries, backoff, and dead-letter handling
- Scheduled and recurring jobs
- Idempotency and observing job status

**Hands-on:** Move a slow task (e.g. sending email or image processing) to a queue with retries.
**You can move on when:** A request enqueues work that a worker processes reliably, with retries on failure.
