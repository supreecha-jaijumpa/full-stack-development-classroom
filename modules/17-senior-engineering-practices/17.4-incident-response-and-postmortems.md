# 17.4 Incident Response & Postmortems

> Module 17 — Senior Engineering Practices · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to coordinate during an outage and turn it into lasting improvement with a blameless postmortem.

- Incident roles: incident commander, communications, operations
- Severity levels and when to escalate or page
- Communicating status to stakeholders during an active incident
- The blameless postmortem: timeline, impact, root cause, contributing factors
- Five whys and avoiding "human error" as a stopping point
- Turning findings into tracked, owned action items

**Hands-on:** Write a blameless postmortem for a hypothetical incident, including timeline, root cause, impact, and concrete action items.
**You can move on when:** Your postmortem identifies systemic causes and produces action items that would prevent recurrence, naming no scapegoat.
