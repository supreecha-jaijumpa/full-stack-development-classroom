# 17.6 Mentoring & Communication

> Module 17 — Senior Engineering Practices · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to grow other engineers and communicate technical decisions to any audience.

- Tailoring a message to the audience: engineer, PM, executive
- Asking questions that teach instead of just giving answers
- Giving and receiving feedback effectively
- Pairing and unblocking without taking over the keyboard
- Writing async updates that respect the reader's time
- Building trust and psychological safety on a team

**Hands-on:** Write two explanations of the same technical decision — one for an engineer, one for a non-technical stakeholder.
**You can move on when:** You can explain the same decision at two levels of abstraction and each audience would understand it.
