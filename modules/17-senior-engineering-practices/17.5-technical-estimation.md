# 17.5 Technical Estimation

> Module 17 — Senior Engineering Practices · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to estimate engineering work honestly and communicate the uncertainty behind your numbers.

- Breaking work into small, estimable pieces
- Estimating in ranges and confidence levels, not single points
- Naming assumptions, unknowns, and risks that drive the estimate
- Accounting for testing, review, integration, and unknowns
- Why estimates are forecasts, not commitments, and how to say so
- Re-estimating as you learn, and communicating slips early

**Hands-on:** Estimate a multi-part feature with a breakdown, give a range with confidence, and list the assumptions and risks behind it.
**You can move on when:** You can produce an estimate with a stated range and the assumptions that would change it.
