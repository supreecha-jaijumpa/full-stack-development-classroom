# 17.7 Technical Debt Management

> Module 17 — Senior Engineering Practices · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to recognize, quantify, and pay down technical debt as a deliberate engineering decision.

- What technical debt is, and that some of it is intentional and fine
- Distinguishing debt from bad code and from unfinished features
- Quantifying debt by cost (slowed delivery, bugs, risk) and impact
- Making debt visible: tracking, tagging, and prioritizing it
- Paying it down incrementally: boy-scout rule, refactor-with-feature
- Communicating debt trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders

**Hands-on:** Audit a codebase you've worked in, log the top tech-debt items with cost/impact, and propose a prioritized paydown plan.
**You can move on when:** You can justify which debt to pay now and which to defer, in terms a product owner would accept.
