# 17.2 Writing Design Docs / RFCs

> Module 17 — Senior Engineering Practices · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to write a design doc that aligns a team and surfaces problems before any code is written.

- The anatomy of a design doc: context, goals, non-goals, proposal, alternatives, risks
- Writing for the reader: what reviewers need to make a decision
- Documenting alternatives considered and why you rejected them
- Stating trade-offs and open questions honestly
- Using diagrams and concrete examples instead of prose walls
- Driving a doc through review to a decision

**Hands-on:** Write a design doc / RFC for a feature in your capstone, including context, goals, alternatives considered, and a chosen approach.
**You can move on when:** A reader unfamiliar with your feature can understand the problem, the decision, and why you made it from your doc alone.
