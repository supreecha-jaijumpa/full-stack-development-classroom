# 16.2 Scaling Patterns

> Module 16 — System Design & Architecture · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to scale a system as load grows, and the cost/complexity trade-offs of each technique.

- Vertical vs horizontal scaling and when each runs out
- Stateless services and why they scale horizontally with ease
- Caching layers: client, CDN, application, and database; invalidation strategies
- Read replicas, connection pooling, and read/write splitting in PostgreSQL
- Database sharding and partitioning, and the operational pain they introduce
- Load balancing strategies and autoscaling triggers

**Hands-on:** Write a scaling plan for an existing endpoint at 100x traffic, with capacity estimates and placement of caches, replicas, and shards.
**You can move on when:** You can identify the bottleneck for a given workload and propose the cheapest scaling change that resolves it.
