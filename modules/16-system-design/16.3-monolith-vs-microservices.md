# 16.3 Monolith vs Microservices

> Module 16 — System Design & Architecture · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to choose between a monolith and microservices based on team, stage, and operational maturity rather than hype.

- The modular monolith as the sensible default for most products
- Costs microservices add: network calls, distributed transactions, observability, deployment
- Conway's Law and how org structure shapes service boundaries
- Identifying seams: where a monolith can later be split cleanly
- Data ownership per service and the end of cross-service joins
- Migration strategy: strangler fig pattern instead of a rewrite

**Hands-on:** Write a decision memo recommending monolith or microservices for a fictional 5-person startup, justified against team size and stage.
**You can move on when:** You can defend a "start with a modular monolith" position and name the specific signals that justify splitting later.
