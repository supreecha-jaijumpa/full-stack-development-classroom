# 16.6 Designing for Reliability

> Module 16 — System Design & Architecture · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to design systems that degrade gracefully and keep working when parts fail.

- Failure modes: dependency outages, slow responses, partial failures, cascading failures
- Timeouts, retries with exponential backoff and jitter
- Circuit breakers and bulkheads to contain failure
- Graceful degradation and fallback behavior
- Idempotency, redundancy, and health checks
- SLAs, SLOs, SLIs, and error budgets

**Hands-on:** For a service you designed, enumerate failure modes and write the mitigations (timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, fallbacks, idempotency).
**You can move on when:** You can take any external dependency in a design and state what happens to users when it fails and how you contain the blast radius.
