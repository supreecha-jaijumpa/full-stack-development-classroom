# 16.4 Message Queues & Events

> Module 16 — System Design & Architecture · Phase 5

**What you'll learn:** How to decouple systems and absorb load spikes with queues and event-driven architecture.

- Synchronous vs asynchronous communication and when async wins
- Queues vs pub/sub vs event streams (e.g. SQS/RabbitMQ vs Kafka)
- Producers, consumers, and the role of a broker
- Delivery guarantees: at-least-once, at-most-once, exactly-once myths
- Idempotent consumers, dead-letter queues, and retry/backoff
- Event-driven patterns: outbox, event sourcing, and eventual consistency

**Hands-on:** Redesign a synchronous flow (order → email → invoice) as event-driven; diagram producers, consumers, and failure/retry handling.
**You can move on when:** You can explain how a queue protects a downstream service during a traffic spike and how you'd guarantee no duplicate side effects.
