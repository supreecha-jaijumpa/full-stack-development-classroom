# 9.8 NoSQL & When to Use It

> Module 09 — Databases · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How NoSQL stores differ from relational databases and when each is the right tool.

- Categories: document, key-value, wide-column, and graph stores
- Document modeling (e.g. MongoDB) vs. relational tables
- Trade-offs: flexible schema, denormalization, and consistency
- CAP theorem and eventual consistency at a high level
- When relational is the safer default
- Mixing stores in one system (polyglot persistence)

**Hands-on:** Choose one feature from your domain and argue whether PostgreSQL or a NoSQL store fits better.
**You can move on when:** You can name concrete trade-offs behind a SQL-vs-NoSQL decision.
