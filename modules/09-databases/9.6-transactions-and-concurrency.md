# 9.6 Transactions & Concurrency

> Module 09 — Databases · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to keep multi-step writes consistent when many clients hit the database at once.

- ACID properties and what a transaction guarantees
- BEGIN, COMMIT, and ROLLBACK
- Running transactions with Prisma (`$transaction`)
- Isolation levels and the anomalies they prevent
- Race conditions, lost updates, and deadlocks
- Optimistic vs. pessimistic locking strategies

**Hands-on:** Implement a multi-step operation in one transaction and demonstrate it rolling back on failure.
**You can move on when:** Your multi-write operation leaves no partial state when one step fails.
