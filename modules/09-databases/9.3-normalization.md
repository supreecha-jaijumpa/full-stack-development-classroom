# 9.3 Normalization

> Module 09 — Databases · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to structure tables to eliminate redundancy and update anomalies.

- Why redundancy causes insert, update, and delete anomalies
- First normal form (atomic values, no repeating groups)
- Second normal form (no partial dependencies)
- Third normal form (no transitive dependencies)
- Functional dependencies and candidate keys
- When and why to denormalize deliberately

**Hands-on:** Take a flat, redundant table and refactor it step by step into third normal form.
**You can move on when:** You can identify which normal form a table violates and fix it.
