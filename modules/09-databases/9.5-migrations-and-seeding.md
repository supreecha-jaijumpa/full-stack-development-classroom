# 9.5 Migrations & Seeding

> Module 09 — Databases · Phase 3

**What you'll learn:** How to evolve a schema safely over time and populate reproducible data.

- What migrations are and why you never edit a live schema by hand
- Versioned SQL migrations with goose, and why `AutoMigrate` is not enough for production
- Migration history, drift, and resetting in development
- Writing an idempotent seed command in Go
- Seeding realistic test data for development
- Coordinating migrations across a team and in deploys

**Hands-on:** Author a migration for a schema change plus a Go seed command that fills the blog with sample data.
**You can move on when:** A teammate can reset, migrate, and seed your database with documented commands.
