# 7.1 Testing Fundamentals

> Module 07 — Frontend Testing & Quality · Phase 2

**What you'll learn:** Why we test, what to test, and how to choose the right kind of test for each situation.

- The purpose of tests: confidence, regression safety, and documentation.
- The testing pyramid: unit, integration, and end-to-end, and their trade-offs.
- Testing behavior, not implementation details.
- Arrange–Act–Assert structure and writing clear test names.
- Test doubles: stubs, mocks, and fakes, and when to use them.
- What is and isn't worth testing; avoiding brittle tests.

**Hands-on:** Write a short test plan for your dashboard classifying each feature as best covered by unit, component, or E2E tests.
**You can move on when:** You can justify, for a given feature, which level of the testing pyramid it belongs at and why.
