# 7.5 Accessibility Testing

> Module 07 — Frontend Testing & Quality · Phase 2

**What you'll learn:** How to make your app usable by everyone and verify it with automated and manual accessibility checks.

- Why accessibility matters: users, law, and overall UX quality.
- Core principles: semantic HTML, ARIA roles, and the POUR/WCAG guidelines.
- Keyboard navigation, focus management, and visible focus states.
- Automated audits with axe (browser extension and `@axe-core` in tests).
- Manual checks: screen reader basics, color contrast, and labels.
- Fixing common issues: missing alt text, unlabeled inputs, poor contrast.

**Hands-on:** Run an axe audit on your dashboard, then fix the reported issues until the audit passes.
**You can move on when:** Your app passes an axe audit and is fully operable with the keyboard alone.
