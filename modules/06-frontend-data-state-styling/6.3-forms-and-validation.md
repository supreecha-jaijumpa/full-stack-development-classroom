# 6.3 Forms & Validation

> Module 06 — Frontend Data, State & Styling · Phase 2

**What you'll learn:** How to build forms that validate input, surface clear errors, and stay accessible.

- Controlled vs. uncontrolled inputs and when each is appropriate.
- Managing form state and submission, including disabling submit while invalid.
- Validation strategies: on blur, on change, and on submit.
- Schema validation with a library like Zod and inferring types from the schema.
- Displaying inline error messages and associating them with inputs (`aria-describedby`).
- Handling async submission, loading, and server-side error responses.

**Hands-on:** Build a sign-up form validated by a Zod schema, with inline errors and a submit button disabled until the form is valid.
**You can move on when:** Your form blocks invalid submission, shows field-level errors, and infers its TypeScript types from the schema.
