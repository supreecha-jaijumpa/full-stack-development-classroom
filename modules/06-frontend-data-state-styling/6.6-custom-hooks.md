# 6.6 Custom Hooks

> Module 06 — Frontend Data, State & Styling · Phase 2

**What you'll learn:** How to extract and reuse stateful logic by writing your own custom hooks.

- What makes a function a hook (the `use` prefix and the Rules of Hooks).
- Extracting repeated `useState`/`useEffect` logic into a custom hook.
- Returning values and functions from a hook and typing them.
- Common patterns: `useToggle`, `useLocalStorage`, `useDebounce`, `useFetch`.
- Composing hooks from other hooks (including TanStack Query).
- Keeping hooks pure, focused, and independently testable.

**Hands-on:** Extract data-fetching and form-handling logic into two reusable, typed custom hooks and use them in components.
**You can move on when:** You can move duplicated logic out of two components into one custom hook without changing behavior.
